e-File rejection process

This topic applies only to Non Tax Filing clients.

The steps explaining the e-File filing rejection process are listed in the following table:

  1. The e-File submission is identified as Rejected.
  2. The Submission Status is changed to Rejected.
  3. Your original filing is reset from Rejected to an Active state, and an e-mail is sent to your e-mail account.
  4. On the Home page, in the Tasks section, a rejection task is displayed, called Review and Re-submit e-File/e-Pay Rejections. If there is more than one rejection, only one task is displayed.
  5. Click Review and Re-submit e-File/e-Pay Rejections. On the Payment and Filing Overview page, the rows that had previously been rejected are displayed.
  6. On the Payment and Filing Overview page, a Rejection Info button is displayed. Click this button to view rejection information for a submission.
  7. You can submit an e-Filing.
